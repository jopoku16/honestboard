This supplement holds the tables and the figure that the main text points to but does not need on the page: the record test's false-rejection rate by position in the chain and its power (Section~5.1 of the paper), the ranking lottery (Section~5.4), the full winner's-curse validation tables for SWE-bench Verified, Terminal-Bench~2 and LiveBench (Section~5.3), and the record ladders of five leaderboards (Section~6).
Every number here is generated by the same script as the main text (\texttt{make\_numbers.py} in the repository, \url{https://github.com/jopoku16/honestboard}), from the same result files.
